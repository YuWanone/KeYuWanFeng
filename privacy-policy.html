Privacy Policy

App: CogniVault Offline
Package name: com.cognivault.brainoffline
Effective date: June 12, 2026
Last updated: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CogniVault Offline (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our Android application. CogniVault Offline is an offline brain-training and puzzle app. We designed it so that your activity stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Summary: We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. The app works without an account, without ads, and without internet access for its core features.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, or store on our servers:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or account credentials
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, videos, or other files on your device
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Payment or financial information
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry sent to us

CogniVault Offline does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, social login, or cloud databases operated by us.

2. Information stored locally on your device

To provide app functionality, the following data is stored only on your device using local storage (Room database and DataStore):

  • Training session results, scores, and statistics
  • Puzzle level progress, star ratings, and saved puzzle states
  • Achievement unlock status
  • App preferences such as theme, sound, daily goal, and default difficulty
  • Onboarding completion status

This local data is used to show your progress, history, streaks, and settings inside the app. We do not access or receive this data because the app has no backend and no network permission for its core operation.

3. Optional sharing by you

The app includes an optional Export summary feature on the Progress screen. If you choose to use it, the app prepares a plain-text summary and opens your device’s system share sheet. Any sharing (for example, by email or notes) is initiated by you and handled by the app or service you select. We do not receive that shared content.

4. Android permissions

CogniVault Offline declares no user-facing Android permissions in its manifest. The app does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or notification permissions.

Android system libraries used by the app may include internal, signature-level permissions that are not shown to users on the Google Play Store and are not used to access your personal data.

5. Network and offline operation

CogniVault Offline is designed to work fully offline. Training, puzzles, progress tracking, and settings do not require Wi‑Fi or mobile data. The app does not transmit your local progress to us.

6. Android backup and device transfer

The app supports Android’s backup and device-transfer features. If you enable backup on your device, your local app data (such as preferences and progress) may be backed up by Google to your personal Google account or transferred when you move to a new device, according to your device and Google account settings.

That backup process is managed by Android/Google on your device. We do not operate those backup services and do not receive your backup data.

7. Data deletion

You can delete local app data at any time:

  • In the app: More → Settings → Clear local data
  • On your device: Uninstall the app, which removes locally stored app data from that device

Deleting local data cannot be undone. Because we do not store your data on our servers, we cannot restore deleted data for you.

8. Children’s privacy

CogniVault Offline is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will address the request.

9. Google Play Data safety

For Google Play Console purposes, we declare that the app does not collect or share user data with the developer. Local app activity is stored on the device for functionality and is not transmitted to us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the in-app Privacy Policy where applicable.
